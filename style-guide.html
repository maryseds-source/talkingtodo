Usage Rule

Bubblegum is the dominant canvas. Barbie Pink is reserved for high-energy focal points and the outer chrome. Plum Noir provides the thick doll-house outline across every element — never pure black. White and Powder appear only inside cards or speech bubbles. Malibu Blue is reserved for Ken props and playful secondary moments; Glitter Gold is a garnish, never a fill. Never place magenta text on pink without a white or plum stroke — it must sparkle.

Component Rule

Every interactive element gets the full treatment: 3px Plum Noir outline, hard offset shadow, pill or rounded shape. On press, elements sink into their shadow. Corners are never sharp — if it could scratch a doll, round it off.

Hearts, sparkles, stars, bows, heels, palm trees, and roller skates are the core motif set. Motifs are scattered as stickers at a slight rotation (−6° to +6°), always on a white or powder tile with the standard outline. Maximum three motifs per composition — sparkle, don’t clutter.

05 — Voice & Tone

Say this

  • “Hi Barbie! Your dream closet just restocked.”
  • “This look? Iconic. This price? Even more iconic.”
  • “You can be anything — today you’re early.”

Never this

  • Sarcasm, irony, or anything “too cool to care”
  • Beige language: “utilize”, “leverage”, “solutions”
  • All-lowercase minimalism — Dreamhouse capitalises joy
